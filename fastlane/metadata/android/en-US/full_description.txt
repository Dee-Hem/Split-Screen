Splitz lets you pair any two installed apps and launch them together in split screen with a single tap from your home screen or within the app. Organize your favorite pairs into custom folders for quick access.

Accessibility Service Disclosure:
Splitz uses Android's Accessibility Service API exclusively to automate placing the two selected apps into Android's native split-screen mode.

Important:
• The Accessibility Service must be enabled for automatic split-screen placement.
• Without the Accessibility Service enabled, Splitz will open the two apps sequentially one after another, but cannot automatically place them into split screen.
• Privacy & Security: Splitz operates 100% offline with zero network connectivity (no INTERNET permission requested or used). The Accessibility Service does not read screen contents, monitor keystrokes, track personal data, or transmit any information.
